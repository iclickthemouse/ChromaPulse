Quick guide

Tonal preserves more of a photograph's shading with variable dots. Indexed makes flat color blocks; use dot gain or switch to Tonal for more visible screening.

LPI controls screen density, while DPI controls the pixel resolution of exported films. Change garment color to preview how overprinting and underbase affect the design.

Plate view shows actual separation dots at the chosen scale. Downloaded PNG plates use transparency for unprinted pixels and black for printed pixels.
